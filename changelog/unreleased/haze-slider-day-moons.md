**Haze has a slider, and day moons fade into the sky.** Settings now has a HAZE slider,
from none to twice the usual thickness, on top of the day's Weather. In Targ, a moon in the
daytime sky is washed toward the sky behind it: its dark side vanishes and its lit side
shows as a pale crescent.
