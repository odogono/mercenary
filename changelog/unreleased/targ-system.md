**Targ's Enhanced sky is read from an authored Targ System.** TARG, its sun and its
two moons now follow the Gamma System's rules: the Bodies stay put and only TARG
turns, so the sun, moons and stars cross the sky once a day. Each moon keeps one
phase and the sun keeps one height through the year. The opening still begins at
9:00 with the outer moon in view. The `obliquityDegrees` and `yearDays` sky settings
are gone, and old settings files that still name them load as before (#942).
