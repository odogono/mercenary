**Weather varies the Haze on Bodies with air.** In Enhanced presentation each local day
is Clear, Hazy or Mist, picked from the Body and the day. Every morning starts misty and
clears toward the day's Weather by noon. The `weather` console command shows it, or pins
it with `weather clear|hazy|mist` until `weather auto` (#959).
