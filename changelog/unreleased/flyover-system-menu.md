**Escape pauses the Flyover under its own System Menu.** The Camera and the
Targ clock stand still until you resume. LEAVE FLYOVER returns to the start
page and takes the Flyover out of the browser history. On Targ, the menu sets
the day length and the surface's wireframe and road weight and glow. These
choices last until reload and never change the game's preferences (#188).
