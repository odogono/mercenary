**A Shrine stands off the city map, in Surface Cell 20,20.** Coming near it
shows a message, anything left inside it is acknowledged, and an
energy crystal left there is accepted as an offering and fixed in place for
good. It is the first Authored Asset placed in Targ's world, and older Saves
still load with it standing there. Its placement names the Talk Script
that answers for it, which hears only what happens there.
