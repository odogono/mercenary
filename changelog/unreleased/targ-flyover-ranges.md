**The Targ Flyover reads Coastal and Surface Range.** Below one Targ radius the
readout changes from ORBITAL to COASTAL, then COASTAL / SURFACE in the dual
band, then SURFACE. Outside Orbital it shows longitude, latitude, signed
altitude in Targ radii and the city's surface X/Z. City Island now stands at
34°N, where the game's Targ sky is drawn from. Drawing the surface comes later
(#187).
