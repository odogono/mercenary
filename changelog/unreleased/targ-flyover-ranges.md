**The Targ Flyover reads Coastal and Surface Range on the Targ panel.** The
Flyover now shows the game's own Targ panel instead of the Damocles one: the
grid reference under the camera, its height and speed in the game's scale, and
the Range (ORBITAL, COASTAL, COASTAL / SURFACE or SURFACE) in the Benson
window. A line above the panel gives longitude, latitude, surface X/Z and
altitude in Targ radii. City Island now stands at 34°N, where the game's Targ
sky is drawn from. Drawing the surface comes later (#187).
