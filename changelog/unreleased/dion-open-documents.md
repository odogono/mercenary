**Mercenary III opens what you carry.** Keypad Enter opens the head object's panel on the centre box, with its recovered art: MARGARETS LETTER and the game's 26 other documents, the switches, the cards. `+`/`-` page a document and `*` reads the current item into the message bar row by row. Dropping the open object closes it. Panels whose effects are not ported yet open but note their keys in the Shell Console (#756).
