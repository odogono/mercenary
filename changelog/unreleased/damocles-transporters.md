**Damocles's Transporters work.** Walk into one and you come out at the next
available Transporter round the ring, at wherever it now lies. Which half of
the compass you walk in facing sets the direction; walking backwards reverses
it. Standing in one, a digit key `0`–`9` goes straight to slot 64 + n. The
panel shows VOID while you stand in a cube, and a Transporter the ring passes
over raises `INTERMEDIATE TRANSPORTERS NOT AVAILABLE` (#998).
