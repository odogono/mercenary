**A carried Damocles Transporter's digit takes you into VOID.** Standing in
another Transporter, its digit puts you inside the carried Transporter's own
cube, on the column where the original parks what you carry, at a height set by
its place in the Carried Stack. A Transporter left in the BRIEFCASE goes to the
column beyond it. From there, backing out and in again continues round the
ring, and any digit takes you on. A Save made there restores you to the same
cube. What VOID looks like around the cube is still to come (#1017, #1022).
