**Mercenary III's lifts carry the player between Floors.** In a lift car, press a
Floor key: `1`–`9`, `0` or `B` for Floor 10, `G` for the ground floor. The doors
close and the Floor Label beside them steps one Floor at a time. Then the doors
open on the new Floor. A Floor the building lacks, or the one already shown, is
refused. A Save keeps the Floor and a ride in progress, and the console's
`floor N` moves at once, as in Damocles (#835).
