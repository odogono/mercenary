**Enhanced Targ draws through the shared planner.** Targ's Enhanced surface and Rooms are
now drawn by the same planner and renderer as Damocles and Mercenary III. Wireframes are
depth-tested against faces and each other, distant buildings fade into the sky's horizon
colour by TARG's Atmosphere instead of turning grey, and wireframe weight and glow use the
shared stroke settings. Authentic Targ is unchanged (#954).
