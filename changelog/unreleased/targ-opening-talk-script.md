**Targ's opening is now a Talk Script.** Benson's crash report and the
Dominion Dart sales pitch say the same lines in the same order. The pauses
between them run on their own clock, and each offer gives the player five
seconds to press Y. Once the pitch ends, Benson is free at once, so the
briefing rooms speak without waiting for the player to board the Dart. A
Save taken during the opening by an earlier version keeps its place in
the game but skips the rest of the opening (#844).
