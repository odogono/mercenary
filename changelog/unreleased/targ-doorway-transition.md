**Taking a Doorway in Targ wipes the view as the Atari ST does.** Play pauses for about a
second while, in the Authentic view, one Room's flat fill sweeps up or down the 3D view over
the other Room's frame. The panel and sound keep running. The Enhanced view pauses for the same
time without the wipe (#976).
