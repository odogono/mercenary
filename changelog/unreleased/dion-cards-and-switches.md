**Dion's cards show their recovered text, and its switches drive their effects.**
A TO Z follows the location label, PULVIN DETECTOR sounds near ore, and
ELECTROMAGNET stops active fruit-machine reels. Both switches show the original
OFF/ON field. BUS PASS and A TO Z ignore keypad keys as the original does;
bus travel and its next-stop updates remain unported. Device timing and random
state survive Save/Load in schema6; older Dion Saves are refused (#795).
