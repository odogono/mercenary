digit now lands you in the INVENTORY room, on the Floor of its place in the
Carried Stack, or in the BRIEFCASE room for one left in the case. Each Floor
shows the one carried object whose place it is, the walls stop you where the
original does, and the lift joins the Floors (#1022).
