**The EAGLE in Mercenary III turns, pitches and flies as fast as the Damocles Eagle.**
Its craft pass ran once every eleven ticks, the taxi's frame, and so answered the
stick at about a third of the original's rate. While the EAGLE is flown a frame is
now four ticks, as the original ran it in open flight, and eleven everywhere else.
Saves made before the change still load (#837).
