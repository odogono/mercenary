**The Targ Flyover flies from orbit down into the city without a cut.** TARG's
Globe now shows its ocean, City Island, Second City Island and their sand
coastline. Below a tenth of a Targ radius, the city fades in over the Globe as
the game's own Enhanced view: the structures, roads and objects where the Data
Payload places them, on City Island's ground in the ocean, under the Targ sky.
Below a twentieth of a radius it is the surface alone. Nothing in the city
moves, and objects outside the city are left out for now (#187).
