## Separate #756 scope decision proposed for review

The proposed delivery retains D2's destinations/routes/free waiting and adds original Take, Drop and both rotations with equivalent keys/pointer Accommodation. Its candidate inventory includes the initial cards/device and naturally acquired letter, mailbox, cobweb and BLACK BEACON with their source behavior. It preserves refusals and allows successful mutable placement where evidenced; it does not convert support/scenery into fixed objects to simplify state. KEY181's natural acquisition and resulting successful native EAGLE Board require a **scope decision before this proposal can be accepted**. D2 excludes self-piloting; the original input model can now enter that craft. BLUE BEACON remains a natural-route gate; BLACK BEACON's complete held/variant/world-reader effects remain a closure gate.

This is an inventory proposal, **not an accepted whitelist**. The broad plan says interaction at already playable locations; therefore objects that ordinary input can select there cannot be excluded just by disabling their handlers. If additional takeable residents, building hooks, craft access or service effects are inseparable from the input model, record them and return to scope review. A positional clamp, invisible interaction boundary, different held-bit rule or silent craft-key suppression is not established original behavior. [Programme proposed scope](mercenary-iii-object-interaction-plan.md#proposed-scope), [D2 admission boundaries](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-milestone-review.md#decisions).

No evidence here admits Bil reception, solicitor Floor1/Floor9, HQ/lifts, Transporter, bus network, return TOURCRAFT, onward taxi/service, self-piloting or foreign campaign branches. CHILD216, PCB218 and ORE127 remain outside this selected object set. R2's finite graphs establish their mechanisms, not ordinary access; [R2 effects/ordinary limits](mercenary-iii-object-interaction-r2-effects.md#owners-and-scope-limits) states the consequence if R3 later admits them. A held/key-conditioned native EAGLE route is precisely the kind of new consequence that must not be hidden by that exclusion list.
