They run at 32 MHz, use the debugger-fed key latch `$56726`, and do not test physical keyboard/IKBD delivery. The prior route's16/32 MHz agreement is not a CPU comparison of these new families.

`placement_probe.py` records Drop entry `$55794`, accepted placement just after the Residence/group/zone stores at `$559C6`, and either silent return `$5577A` or rank/height refusal branch `$55788`. Captures at `$55788` precede the string9 helper; next/later top captures cover subsequent progression. It traces the original candidate writer `$545D6`, support-height reader `$558AA` and ordinary Take dispatch. No candidate list or selection is injected after the initial Drop event. Re-pick at top 162 writes **only** the Take scancode, after twenty original simulation frames.

The read-only `placement_verify.py` passes **264 explicit assertions across nine families**, with **105 eleven-tick gaps, 1,388 sampled in-frame phase checks and 1,283 masked-register checks**. It checks pins, action counts, exact subsequent-input absence, original selected keys, complete head placement/staging state and the stated refusal outcomes. Three corruption controls on isolated copies reject a changed post-Take zone, a changed refusal-cut Residence byte and an added later selection injection; the intact copy passes before/after those controls. `placement_observer_checks.py` and ignored `placement/observer-checks.json` retain these checks. It does not implement or simulate the handlers. These are sampled checks, not a proof of every owner or instruction.

## Placement zone survives re-pick

The direct contact fixture places TAXICARD with the same full surface Residence and group as the support controls, but **zone 1**. The head's previous staged zone was 4. After Take, state `$88`, staging XYZ `(33237,0,99100)`, group49 and Residence `[120,1,0,16]` return; **zone remains 1** at both later cuts. Support re-pick retains zone 4.

Thus equal carried identity, held state and staging Residence do not establish equal placement-zone state. A Save contract cannot silently derive all staged owners from stack membership or discard this difference. This is a retained finite observation; it does not by itself prove that every subsequent reader exposes that zone difference. Browser Save admission and all later futures still need their own review and comparisons.

Sources: `direct-contact-native-retake/{drop-entry,placed,f22,f25}.ram`, the corresponding support cuts and the ledger's `head104` observations. The original direct zone writer is `$559A0`; the inspected staging writer range is `$53CC4..$53D32`.
