# Mercenary III TOURCRAFT: Damocles reuse assessment

Research handoff for [R6 / #707](https://github.com/odogono/mercenary/issues/707), 2026-10-02. This note compares the current independent implementation with the used METIS resident-13 → TOLOSA flight. It supplies the reuse part of R6, not the separate original-engine timing confirmation or a runtime implementation. The [D2 decision](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-milestone-review.md) and [ADR 0065](../adr/0065-promote-bounded-dion-catalogue-content-through-an-explicit-export.md) govern the selected slice; [#563](https://github.com/odogono/mercenary/issues/563) and its [cross-title Mover note](craft-mover-across-titles.md) are the comparison starting point.

The evidence supports extracting Damocles's arithmetic, common craft pass, support/box predicates and space numeric pass. It also establishes concrete reasons to retain Dion playback, frame orchestration, nearest-Body range, automated landing adaptation, admission, clock, scripts and Body binding. The existing Damocles `spaceFrame` and Gameplay Session cannot be imported as the Dion flight. Static correspondence is strong evidence for reuse, but it does not replace the required Dion pass-by-pass and 50 Hz/eleven-tick probes.

## Differences that affect the selected flight

**Playback is not the existing self-piloted input adapter.** MIII's surface `$59014..$59042` and space `$59516..$59544` entries read the pointer at `$709A`; compare one stream byte with the **low frame-counter byte** `$7181`; require the second byte's high bit; consume a two-byte entry; retain its low seven control bits in `$7057`; and use that latch for the pass. Original Damocles's surface `$57F50..$57F7C` and space `$587E0..$5880C` instead compare a **word frame counter**, test a word at `+2`, consume four bytes and retain the last byte. The current independent Damocles `craftPass`/`spacePass` accept an explicit stick; they implement neither playback decoder. A Dion decoder must own its cursor, retained control, counter wrapping and phase writers and then supply the proven numeric pass. Command/phase records outside these control preambles belong to R6's full used-stream recovery. [S; I, `craftPass`, `spacePass`; R5 launch/playback findings]

Live input nevertheless has a separate cabin-view role: `$5183E` reads `$7056`, and bits 3/2 change `$717A` by ±16 during playback; `$59D34..$59DD0` applies that offset to the copied presentation pose/yaw, while the numeric craft yaw remains `$7156`. Ordinary exit `$55718` copies the presentation yaw `$7136` back to `$7156`. Therefore “playback owns movement” does not imply “ignore all live stick” or a single yaw word for the craft, view and on-foot exit. [R6's live-input control](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-playback.md) confirms that right held over 91 passes produces `$05B0` (=91×16), retains the same entry/contact/recorded-L frames and XYZ/cash endpoint, and changes on-foot yaw from `$FD79` to `$0329`; the subsequent held-down bit does not change cabin yaw. Clock seconds differ by one under that original-engine instrumentation, so the input experiment is not evidence of an exact atomic-tick clock. [S, these reader/writers; R6 live-input control]

`$51860..$51864` holds the stable long 64 in the inspected admission, launch, craft, space, arrival/descent and on-foot R5 captures. Its only absolute program references found are the readers `$51878` and `$59DAA`: the service publisher places resident Y at craft Y +64−8, while the cabin presentation adds 64 to the surface copied-eye/altitude values. Selector 2 returns before that latter surface-height adjustment. No live input writer of this offset was found; do not infer free vertical cabin movement from the held stick. [S, named captures and reader scan]

**The Dion frame contains service work between movement and collision.** At `$5A6B4` it dispatches the craft Mover; `$5A6B8` calls `$518F0`; `$5A6BE` publishes the pose; `$5A6C2` tests boxes. A refusal then restores XYZ, negates throttle/speed mantissas, dispatches a second pass at `$5A6E2`, publishes again and writes the stop exponent. There is no second `$518F0` between that retry and the stop. `craftFrame` currently packages a Damocles pass, landing and collision with no Dion service callback. Reusing the whole wrapper requires proving the interposed Dion work cannot change the numeric/collision inputs at each used phase; extracting `craftPass` and the predicates lets Dion preserve the evidenced order directly. [S, `$5A68C..$5A732`; I, `craftFrame`, `DionSession.frame`, `DamoclesSession.tickCraft`]

**Space range is computed before rotation in MIII.** MIII `$53D6A..$53E0A` converts the unrotated Body-minus-craft X/Y/Z longs, squares them, adds X² and Y² before Z², takes the square root and updates nearest range/Body. Only afterward does `$53E12` rotate for drawing. Damocles `$52E1C` rotates first, and its `bodyRange` consumes the rotated rows. Real Euclidean invariance is insufficient: this arithmetic rounds table products/additions, so the algorithms need not leave the same range word or arrival verdict. Preserve a Dion unrotated range adapter; do not reuse `bodyRange` or `spaceFrame` unchanged. The shared arithmetic still performs its products/sums/root. Both original scans retain the arrival hold and nearest extent-exponent test, but Dion's full Body table, scan order and phase state must be supplied by R6/R7. [S, range ledgers; I, `bodyRange`, `spaceFrame`]

**Automated landing omits Body rotation subtraction.** MIII `$4EF3C..$4EF48` tests playback `$70A2`; while active, it skips subtracting the destination rotation word `$575A(a0)` from the longitude before converting longitude into X. Damocles's `landingPoint` always subtracts the high word of `turn`. Latitude, pole handling, angular conversion and coordinate shifts otherwise align. This is a used, concrete Dion difference. A small pure adapter can call/extract the proven latitude/angle/place math and explicitly pass zero rotation for playback, if full boundary comparisons verify that substitution; retain the true rotation for sky and subsequent play. Do not reset the destination's real rotation accumulator to zero. [S, MIII `$4EF02..$4F01C`; I, `landingPoint`, `craftAtan2`, `subStellarLongitude`]

**Space entry and Body arrival have title-local effects.** MIII `$56B28` makes the same selector 2, arrival hold 50, exponent and level-pitch writes as Damocles `$55868`, but adds `$51834`'s active-playback service publisher among its pose/presentation calls. MIII `$5A15E` retains the over-Body altitude `$1800000`, coordinate conversion, Body/context binding and support tail, removes Damocles's `body + $1F` landing-script trigger, invokes the service publisher, and writes `$6FF48` according to destination: `$FFFF` for selector `$64` (TOLOSA), `$FFF3` for `$68`, `$FFF7` for `$54`, `$FFFB` for `$50`. These other destinations are comparison facts, not admitted journeys. `$6FF48` is a future-affecting moving-object programme request, as the next paragraph establishes; it must not be discarded as presentation. [S, space-entry/Body-arrival ledger; I, `enterSpace`, `land`, `landingTrigger`, `DamoclesSession.landCraft`]

**TOLOSA activates the OBSERVER moving-object programme.** Reader `$523E0..$52448` checks flag `$7271` bit 6 first; when set, `$511A8` clears the request and pool residents 56–59. Otherwise a negative request starts timer `$6E86` at 1000, clears the request word, complements its value to select a paired-byte programme at `$6E50`, then stores its count at `$6E8D`, cursor at `$6E88` and next request byte at `$6FF49`. TOLOSA's `$FFFF` selects the first bytes `03 01 07 FF`: its first count is 3 and its live request becomes 1. The positive-request path selects the lists at `$523A0` or `$523B4/$523BC/$523CA` and updates their resident records through `$520CC`. The three resident state words at `$5572/$5576/$557A` belong to indices 56/57/58 (base `$5492`, stride 4), not to the numeric craft's selected slot. The [object recovery](dion-object-geometry.md) identifies `$52090`'s bindings of these residents as model 321 ATTACK SHIP or 320 OBSERVER. [S, request/readers/table; object-recovery writer table]

In the R5 confirmation captures from `v29000.ram` through `v35000.ram`, `$6FF48` remains `0001`; the programme timer/cursor/count are live, and the three resident state words are nonzero and change. Thus this effect is on the actual admitted flight and persists through on-foot arrival. R5 already lists encounter/shooting consequences in its [campaign inventory](mercenary-iii-services-progression.md); this direct inspection establishes that the TOLOSA arrival writer actually engages the programme. It does not prove a shot, hit, campaign penalty or death occurs on this flight. R6 must retain/recover the used programme and its interaction guards or return an inseparability conflict to D2. R7 needs its actor/geometry coverage, and R8 needs request, programme bytes/cursor/count/timer and live resident state. Damocles's `encounterPass` is a comparison target, but its recovered resident-8/11/15 pool and single encounter gate differ materially from this multi-programme resident-56–59 state. No direct pool/schema reuse is supported. [S, those R5 captures; I, `encounter.ts`; R5 encounter inventory]

The first three fields are signed words, followed by byte shift/delay; each four-record stream has a zero-word terminator and long loop pointer. The used selector list at `$523A0` binds precisely those three paths. Reader `$5227C..$522BC` applies the offsets, produces/stores the velocity through `$52576/$52554`, stamps the resident with the Body and a negative `$8000 | delay` state, and invokes hostile-shot launch `$52448` **only when the shift byte is negative**. All twelve selected shifts are 4, 5 or 6, so this selected phase cannot start that launcher. `$52030` translates these residents by the player's frame XYZ delta; it writes resident positions, not the player's craft pose. These are moving actors to retain, not an authored sky decoration. [S, selected path/list and readers]

Incoming-shot hit processing `$524B8` starts by checking residents 53–55 (offsets `$D4/$D8/$DC`) and returns per resident if its state word is zero. Their state words, and player-shot residents 50–52, are zero in all the inspected R5 craft/TOLOSA/descent/on-foot captures. Player firing `$511D8` also refuses during the service because `$6EB2` is nonzero, and refuses after ordinary leave because craft slot `$7144` is negative. That excludes the player-fire/kill route to decrementing the first programme's count during this bounded automatic journey. The selected phase's negative-shift launcher guard excludes its incoming-hit route; the observed shots remain absent. Consequently no hit-driven craft response, money penalty or programme advance to the later `07 FF` phase is established for this natural first flight. This conclusion is conditional on the recovered service/on-foot guards, zero initial shot records and no intervening script replacing the request; late campaign overrides remain R6's separate gate. It does not justify disabling actor updates, dropping their mutable counts or guaranteeing all later TOLOSA play is noncombat. [S, guards/state reads and named R5 captures; R5 campaign inventory]

**Ground contact and automatic exit are separate used-path events.** The [R6 eleven-frame-end trace](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-playback.md) reaches ordinary contact after the pass at top-frame 883, observed at top-frame 884; its recorded playback L invokes `$5569E` at frame 927, with no earlier leave at touchdown. These are this instrumented trace's frame labels, not accepted exact atomic-tick counts. Thus automatic exit means no user L is required, not that this ordinary touchdown directly disembarks. Dion must preserve the recorded command and its admission/contact/cleanup order. Separately, the static **hard-touchdown alternative** MIII `$58DB6..$58E28` has the same two box refusals, ground/level/stop shape and land-versus-sea branch as Damocles `$57CF2..$57D64`, with relocated title-local calls and sound programme addresses; it can invoke Dion leave `$5569E` and the `$52726` join. Do not claim that alternative is the used TOURCRAFT exit. The independent Damocles `craftFrame` returns `landed` for a hard touchdown and `DamoclesSession.touchDown` supplies its sea/sound/leave effects. Dion still needs its own playback-command leave, placement and service cleanup.
