## Ordinary set and unresolved effects

The [R3 proposal](mercenary-iii-object-interaction-r3.md#resident-and-per-object-admission-inventory) supplies ordinary LETTER220, MAILBOX219, COBWEB124, KEY181 and BLACK BEACON165 acquisitions in addition to initial104/103/135. None matches the three pickup-response table IDs. That bounded no-match fact follows from Dion's table, and does not make these objects inert: R2's descriptions/Head Commands, live held predicates and admitted world/service readers still require their own closure. R1's script-only held KEY A38 remains outside the stack in every selected effect capture; the observer checks it, rather than deriving held state from inventory membership.

There is still no ordinary acquisition witness for CHILD216, PCB218 or ORE127. Their synthetic execution cannot silently promote them, the PCB repeating future or ORE political/model alternatives. If R3 admits any of them, the delivery must review the resulting source readers, world/model changes, campaign alternatives and persistence consequences. The [R1 audit](mercenary-iii-object-interaction-r1-audit.md) and R3's route/scope gates retain their separate responsibilities.

Explicit unexecuted boundaries remain: ORE with existing flags10/23 and its counterfactual flag22-clear successor; CHILD poll clamp edges; all scheduler/availability combinations beyond the matrix; negative description cleanup; complete cross-readers of ORE's political flags and swapped model table; exact interaction sound/pixels; original game disk Save/Load after these pickups; browser save/rebuild and resumed futures. Read-only replay and whole-machine continuation establish the original mechanism; they certify no future browser codec. This pickup audit is therefore a bounded acceptance result, not a claim that every #771 owner or the final first-delivery set is closed.

## Reproduction and pins

```sh
python3 research/mercenary-iii-st/scripts/object-interaction-r2-effects-audit.py
```
