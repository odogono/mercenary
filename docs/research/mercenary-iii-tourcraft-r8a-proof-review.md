## Reference order: proved conditional algorithm, missing oracle

The departure selector first asks whether a complete matching history ends at its current departure prefix. A feasible exact prefix sorts before every extension. Otherwise, existence of an extension whose next departure is at most `x` is monotone in `x`; binary search finds the least next departure and repeats. Input is consumed once per frame, so a source with capture frame `F` has at most `F` successful Starts with pre-Start values in `[0,F-1]`. The selector therefore covers empty, repeated and multi-trip schedules under the same complete-source oracle. Unresolved queries propagate as unresolved results. [Selector](../../research/mercenary-iii-st/scripts/taxi-canonical-reference.ts), [pinned input/frame ownership](https://github.com/odogono/mercenary/blob/e4cba434069ca1fbec6e72b3a99cbf775f1663c4/web/src/dion/session.ts)

The product graph must retain the **whole departure vector** in its key. Retaining just one smallest prefix per historical node is unsound: `[] < [10]`, but appending the common later departure 20 gives `[20] > [10,20]`. Once the least accepted vector is fixed, minimum operation-word length and lexicographic operand order define a unique reference even when many inputs reproduce it. Old-node deduplication alone still suffices for existence; it does not suffice for the departure-vector order or for equality of expanded meanings. [Product-graph and word-selection rule](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-source-eligibility.md#direct-decision-procedure-and-safe-reductions)

The regression exhausts all 256 subsets of the eight departure vectors over three synthetic frames and compares selection with sorting. This is strong evidence for ordering logic, including prefix termination and unresolved results. Its oracle is a supplied finite vector list. No test in that file answers complete-source reachability from an arbitrary encoded old record, or selects the shortest/lexicographic operation word after selecting the vector. [Selector regression](../../web/test/dion-canonical-reference.test.ts)

For the retained late source, the calibrated single-trip reset equation yields Starts 16,156, 81,692 and 147,228, and all three independently replay to its complete old source. This proves the first is least **inside that calibrated family**. The `+484` offset and prefix assumptions do not exclude another matching itinerary, call/boarding history, restore history or elapsed-call grouping. Therefore the finite-family selected reference is not yet proved globally canonical under the full order. The same distinction applies to choosing the earlier member of either retained early collision.
