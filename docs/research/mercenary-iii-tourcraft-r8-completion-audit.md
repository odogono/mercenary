## Owner completeness and bounded schema

The field contract covers phase/input/previous candidate, playback and its next-unread records, raw craft/System and collision carries, Location and copied/sampled view, world residences/model bindings, inherited taxi/doors/panel, VM/ordered live stack/availability/queue/signed timer, message progression, cash/passive programme, all fourteen pool slots 50–63 and three trajectories, clock/all 32 Body phases/cached sky, pilot/sign/articulation, both face cursor pairs and the shared selection tag, and logical sound progression plus pending delivery ownership. These are saved progressed values, not requests to infer their state from elapsed time or current presentation. Original byte/word/long bits retain their unsigned widths and recovered signed readers. [Field table and sampled carries](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-snapshot-fields.md#capture-fields-writer-domains-and-cross-field-rules), [primary own-title observer](../../research/mercenary-iii-st/m3lab/snapshot_owners.py), [inventory](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-state-inventory.md#mutable-owners-to-serialize)

The schema retains `snapshot: { remainder, snapshot: state }`, reserves version 2 and admits only the pinned programme and complete served expanded content identity. Its fixed widths, exact array lengths, admitted content-bound references and no-clamping/no-implicit-fill rule are concrete validation requirements. The documentation reconciliation firms the previously proposed ceiling to **262144 UTF-8 bytes** for capture and admission, with at most 512 pending batches, 64 writes per batch and 48 message cells. Fractional remainder remains finite in `[0,0.02)`. These are schema design bounds; a valid session exceeding a bound must produce an explicit failure, without truncation or lost events. Their practical sufficiency remains a codec/capture test. [Bounded representation](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-snapshot-fields.md#representation-and-admission), [ADR 0026](../adr/0026-store-explicit-save-snapshots-as-bounded-json.md)

The important nonzero and independent opening values are preserved: source tick29/frameword1 versus new public counters0; idle timer65507; pool budget5 and inactive nonzero coordinates/residences; METIS phase27376; tick articulation7424 versus sampled angle0; sampled view versus numeric pose; VM PC `$6FF57` and ordered return `[$766D5]`; sound mixer254/slew3840 and the traced register image. The own-title opening supplies these values. Damocles defaults and incidental Catalogue observations supply none of them. [Coherent opening](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-snapshot-fields.md#coherent-new-game-boundary), [seed ledger](../../research/mercenary-iii-st/tourcraft-snapshot-owner-seeds.json), [owner audit](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-snapshot-owner-audit.md)

Opening public counters and delivery ownership are explicit new boundary definitions; original raw addresses are provenance rather than executable Save pointers. Idle/bootstrap null adapters have stated guarded-reader/overwrite conditions. Progressed inactive pilot/sound cursors are retained instead of collapsed back to opening null. Pool/world aliases share one owner or must agree. Immutable Craft Parameter Blocks and excluded random/parameter editors have a writer-domain exclusion, rather than guessed zero overrides. Conservative serialization resolves the unproved cache/omission cases. [Adapters and writer closure](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-snapshot-fields.md#capture-fields-writer-domains-and-cross-field-rules)

The accepted face reconstruction keeps all four cursors and one last-writer tag. `initial` requires complete pre-first-face phase consistency, not zero cursors or original frameword0. Both outputs are derived before any candidate scan, token expansion, inspection or draw, without an invocation, tick or event. Candidate restoration assigns phase/owners first, resolves content references next and reconstructs presentation before committing the replacement. This order preserves the saved candidate and next-unread VM/playback/trajectory/sound boundaries. ## Remaining execution limits and risks

The original controls sample loop-top/message boundaries, not every tick. Late controls inject election seconds and do not establish a naturally waited arbitrary mining/day-rollover restore. Some scalar carries outside the sampled ranges have coherent seeds plus immediate/end full-RAM checks only; they remain serialized. Original first-host screenshot limits, waveform phase and portable backend event delivery remain distinct from state/PSG continuity. A whole machine carries CPU/chip/raster state absent from a browser record, so its successful restore cannot prove independent portable decoding or cache reconstruction. [Extended ledger limits](../../research/mercenary-iii-st/tourcraft-snapshot-restore-evidence.json), [original evidence limits](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-persistence.md#completed-finite-original-engine-controls)

The existing shared Resume writer removes the previous record on encode/write failure; the inherited Dion slot path publishes state and checkpoints before its final draw. The existing `protectResume` shell guard does protect refused-source boot/takeover paths, but those code paths do not meet every schema-2 failure transaction by themselves. #711 must implement and exercise the stronger contract, including nonempty pending event suffixes, repeated envelope writes, failed presentation before commit and preservation after write failure. These are known implementation gates, not additional R8 research omissions. [Shared persistence source](../../web/src/shell/resume.ts), [Dion shell](../../web/src/dion/boot.ts), [sound delivery contract](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-snapshot-fields.md#sound-cursors-and-event-delivery)
