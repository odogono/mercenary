Accepted with research gates, 2026-10-02, for [#704](https://github.com/odogono/mercenary/issues/704). The second slice continues the existing METIS prison/taxi journey: walk from the spaceport taxi exit to resident 13's staffed TOURCRAFT, board, undergo automated launch/space flight/Body entry, and arrive automatically on foot at TOLOSA group 23, cell `01-00`. R5 reproduced that complete path twice from New Game. It uses craft Mover control playback, not the taxi's route loop or self-piloting. Its roughly 60 Hz natural captures do not confirm the browser's eleven-tick cadence.

## R8 compatibility failure returned for review

R8's [persistence contract and original restore comparisons](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-persistence.md), [state inventory](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-state-inventory.md) and [exact I2 source audit](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-taxi-migration.md) expose a conflict in decision 6 after the accepted campaign extension. A naturally produced, fully validated I2 spaceport Save can wait past election/mining while retaining its original cash and script flags; I2 has no campaign threshold reader. Equivalent uninterrupted expanded play must change those fields, including mining's cash clear and the later TOURCRAFT cash guard. Preserving that source and matching that expanded history cannot both hold for these late records.

The user resolved the compatibility priority on 2026-10-02: **match expanded campaign history; allow changes to saved cash/script state**. [ADR 0068](../adr/0068-reconcile-known-taxi-saves-with-expanded-campaign-history.md) records the revised contract. For valid late sources, migration may reconcile these fields through a proved reconstruction of the accepted expanded reference. Preserving them takes lower priority where that comparison requires a change. The audit's exact source identity and validation still apply; source storage bytes remain intact until the replacement transaction succeeds. All 33 early taxi controls retain their full preservation and expanded-future comparison requirements.

This resolves the preservation/equivalence policy conflict, not the reconstruction proof. Fixed cash/flags, campaign effects deferred until Load, blanket taxi-Save refusal, campaign disabling and boarding deadlines remain excluded. R8 must prove deterministic reconstruction against the accepted reference, with its eligibility and canonical selection rule documented. #709 and I4 retain their migration/browser proof gates; the proposed schema 2 and original-machine controls do not certify an independent browser codec. The previously accepted bounds and timing remain in force.

The [late-history ambiguity comparison](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-history-ambiguity.md) now supplies that concrete reconstruction counterexample: two ordinary I2 histories yield identical full source records while R6's admitted scheduler gives different expanded VM states. The user resolved this reference-history decision on 2026-10-02: **use a documented canonical expanded history for ambiguous late records**. Identical validated source state must select the same reference, independently of wall time or storage metadata. Equality with the unknowable actual history is waived for that domain; canonical uninterrupted execution, first-frame restoration and deterministic future comparisons remain required. All 33 early taxi controls retain their full exact comparisons. ADR 0068 records both accepted decisions; the canonical reconstruction algorithm and browser implementation remain to be proved.
