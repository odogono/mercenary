# Mercenary III: pickup, drop and Carried Stack programme

Research and proposed implementation plan for [#756](https://github.com/odogono/mercenary/issues/756), 2026-10-04. The user selected **research and plan first**. Initial evidence and this plan merged in [#769](https://github.com/odogono/mercenary/pull/769). The user subsequently commissioned R1–R3 and starting R1: [R1/#770](https://github.com/odogono/mercenary/issues/770), [R2/#771](https://github.com/odogono/mercenary/issues/771) and [R3/#772](https://github.com/odogono/mercenary/issues/772). R3's final review depends on R1/R2 and the subsequently commissioned [R4/#778](https://github.com/odogono/mercenary/issues/778) native EAGLE closure. This document implements no gameplay and expands no playable admission. The companion [ROM evidence report](mercenary-iii-object-interaction-evidence.md) distinguishes recovered source facts from observed play and lists its remaining probes.

## Proposed scope

Treat #756 as a separate programme following the merged [I4 implementation](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-implementation.md), retaining the METIS opening, both taxi destinations and the outward TOURCRAFT journey. D2's [promotion contract](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-promotion.md#drawing-and-admitted-state-are-separate) explicitly excludes Pick Up admissions even where geometry is drawable. Enabling them needs a reviewed scope addition; it is not an unfinished D2 requirement.

The proposed first delivery adds evidenced Take, Drop and stack rotation to the already playable locations, through the existing keys and equivalent pointer controls. It preserves silent refusals, source ordering, messages, sounds, residence changes and object-conditioned effects. It adds no remote start, new interior access or onward service merely to obtain a test object. The initial self-piloting exclusion was revisited after R3 proved ordinary KEY181 acquisition and native EAGLE3 boarding. On 2026-10-04 the user approved including native EAGLE access in this separate programme and researching flight, landing/crash and resulting dependencies in R4/#778. The original key reader must retain its effect; an artificial boarding denial is not the selected scope. This decision commissions the research and future native access, while exact world-content promotion, effects and Save owners remain subject to closure and final review. Other resulting routes return to scope review with their evidence.

Keep Dion Session State and content local to its Title. Assess reuse against Dion evidence at the level of numeric selection, handling and placement helpers. [ADR 0074](../adr/0074-share-evidenced-craft-passes-with-title-owned-orchestration.md) establishes the existing craft boundary; a consequential additional shared boundary needs its own decision. Damocles behavior is a comparison target, not authority for a Dion rule.

The companion audit makes four distinctions material to this plan:

- KEY A's held bit changes during the opening while its residence and the three-item stack remain unchanged in retained original captures. The snapshot contract must preserve this exception to the first-top seed's held-bit/stack agreement.
- Dion slots 157/158 are POWERGLOVE/ANTIGRAV. Their bypass applies to the bit-5 handling refusal; TOURCRAFT and taxis retain a separate bit-4 refusal. Shared handling must not import Damocles's object identities or craft policy.
- The source pickup-response table names CHILD 216, PCB SEATED 218 and PULVIN ORE 127. Those entries have no promoted gameplay model binding today. The first delivery still needs all ordinary description/head/message readers, but it need not invent a trigger for KEY - EAGLE 9SE or MARGARETS LETTER from Damocles's table.
- New cold-boot debugger-input controls walk outside the prison, Drop and re-pick TAXICARD and BUS PASS, and rotate in both directions. These give a concrete route for direct placement and staging. They do not yet confirm accepted eleven-tick timing, initially resident acquisition, physical keyboard delivery, panel/audio or future card/service behavior.

I4's [owner ledger](mercenary-iii-tourcraft-implementation.md) omits inventory paragraph continuation and constrains object-bit writers to KEY A. It also justifies omitted random-sound priority through object 149 being unacquirable. Audit each bounded omission against the proposed acquired-object set; do not carry its proof into a widened input model unchanged.

## Proposed task sequence

R1–R4 are commissioned child tickets; I1–V1 remain briefs for future tickets after the research/scope gates. Dependencies are explicit so implementation starts only after its rules and scope have been established.

1. **R1 — Close take, drop and stack mechanism evidence.** Extend the companion static audit with pinned original execution probes: selection ties and range boundary; full-stack equality and overflow; fixed/heavy/refusal ordering; stack rotation/staging; ground/contact and support placement; re-pick after Drop. Record register/state/message/sound changes and source/capture hashes. Compare exact Dion routines with the current Damocles functions, then publish a reuse matrix. Separate naturally reached, synthetic and static-only cases. Acceptance: every rule needed by the first delivery has an executed witness or a specifically reviewed bounded exclusion.
2. **R2 — Close pickup effects, held-bit exceptions and Head Commands.** Decode every pickup-response entry and its reachable calls/messages/object writes. Exercise idle, busy, queued, unavailable and competing timed/default campaign conditions at the accepted eleven-tick cadence. Establish stack-head versus open-card ownership, label/reveal/description continuation and Drop behavior for the three initial items. Confirm KEY A's script-only held state. Acceptance: the acquired-object set has a finite admitted effect closure, coherent owners and first-frame/ordered-future witnesses.
3. **R3 — Prove routes and review scope/content.** Freeze ordinary New Game routes to actual witnesses in METIS and, where relevant, after TOURCRAFT arrival. Enumerate all naturally selectable residents, required handling/label/model/message/script/support records and resulting routes/effects. Revisit I4's omissions for that set. Acceptance: a reviewed separate #756 scope and exact promotion selection, with no inferred admission from Catalogue coverage. Depends on R1/R2/R4 for final selection; route work may begin alongside them.
4. **R4 — Close native EAGLE flight and resulting dependencies.** Continue the ordinary KEY181 acquisition and EAGLE3 boarding route. Pin controls, Craft Parameter Block readers/writers, occupancy, key/model/residence, contact/landing, safe leave/reboard, hard touchdown/crash, surface/space and Body entry, economics and clock/script/panel/sound futures. Retain original input prefixes and ordered execution; qualify cadence across same-frame transition reentries. Return exact newly reachable content and effects to R3 and enumerate Save owners. Acceptance: every needed outcome has an executed witness or an explicitly reviewed exclusion, with static and synthetic results identified separately. [R4/#778](https://github.com/odogono/mercenary/issues/778) is commissioned and claimed; its [research handoff](mercenary-iii-eagle-flight.md) records the first findings.
5. **I1 — Promote interaction content.** Extend the title-local exporter/loader and content contract with the reviewed handling, identity, response and presentation dependencies, including initial carried items and coherent object bits. Preserve immutable pins and the single mutable initial source. Acceptance: deterministic regeneration, complete reference admission, malformed/mixed-source refusal, selected-content identity changes and independence from unselected Catalogue records. Depends on R3.
6. **I2 — Implement semantic object state and actions.** Add the Carried Stack and evidenced object-bit/residence/handling owners to the production session; implement Take, Drop and both rotations at the original action/frame boundary. Route object scripts, door readers, rendering and panel head/card/description through those owners. Reuse only the helpers R1's comparison supports. Acceptance: ROM-derived transitions/refusals and action ordering match, held-bit exceptions remain legal, ordinary take/drop/re-pick works and taxi/TOURCRAFT/door semantics remain coherent. Depends on I1/R1/R2/R4; native EAGLE state and orchestration must satisfy R4, rather than substituting the scripted TOURCRAFT passenger path.
7. **I3 — Extend Save and Session Resume.** Version the expanded snapshot and admit only reviewed mutable residence/model/bit combinations; serialize stack order, relevant display/script owners and pending action/target. Restore isolated state and reconstruct presentation before publication without executing acquisition scripts again. Acceptance: first frame/panel and ordered later state/events match uninterrupted play before/after Take, rotation, Drop, re-pick, partial ticks and taxi/flight transitions; incompatible/malformed records and write failures preserve previous records/session. Depends on I2; complete before exposing actions in production.
8. **I4 — Connect keys and pointer Accommodation.** Enable existing Bindings and add stack controls/object targeting through the same session actions. Base targets on Dion's rendered projection, with a reviewed rule for overlap, occlusion and current semantic candidate; a clicked label or wireframe must not bypass Take gates. Apply the shared route/console/menu/ownership gates and checkpoint behavior. Acceptance: key and pointer routes produce equivalent semantic outcomes; canvas scaling, touch slop, pitch/roll, clipped/hidden objects and stale targets are covered. Depends on I2/I3.
9. **V1 — Verify the whole programme.** Replay frozen ordinary routes in production, with key/pointer Take, both rotations, Drop/re-pick, head/card behavior, slot Save/Load and reload Resume. Retain both taxi destinations, all 33 expanded taxi continuation controls, TOURCRAFT flight/arrival, campaign boundary checks, KEY A/door and affected Damocles/shared regressions. Acceptance: required checks pass and remaining original raster/audio/synthetic-probe limits are stated. Depends on I4.
