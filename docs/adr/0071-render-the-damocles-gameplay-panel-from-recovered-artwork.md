# Render the Damocles Gameplay Panel from recovered artwork

The actual Damocles Gameplay Session draws its Benson Panel from the pristine DAM1 artwork and Damocles's own recovered writer data, narrowly superseding ADR 0027's authored-only account for that Session. The payload and independently implemented writers follow [the recovered-panel investigation](../research/damocles-recovered-panel.md) and [the writer-data investigation](../research/damocles-panel-writer-data.md); these remain Working-Authority Evidence under ADR 0043 and ADR 0002, not a whole-game fidelity claim. The Damocles Panel Rendition dressing Targ and Flyover's existing fallback remain authored Display Compositions under ADR 0027. ADR 0072 separately admits recovered ST presentation for Targ's own rendition.

Damocles owns its readout adapters, activation categories, native title bands, centre reveal and mutable palette, including the AUTHORS COMPUTER XOR mask. Only independently implemented bit-plane, glyph, pitch and compass primitives are shared with Mercenary III: all 64 Damocles compass frames are compared exhaustively under the documented phase mapping, and Mercenary III's previous pixel output is pinned. The shared attitude contract from #715 remains the adapter's convention. Pointer and keyboard Accommodations, with semantic readouts and focusable controls beside the canvas, remain part of the Display Composition.

Recovered artwork supplies no missing gameplay. Temperature and power levels remain authored placeholders; the specialised grid font and dynamic computer, switch and detonator content layouts remain explicitly authored within recovered art. The unsupported space-navigation composite retains the previous body-coordinate placeholder with its limitation labelled; speed and altitude/range still use the native source-word rules. The document reader retains the payload's gaps, and the accessible description and panel provenance name these limits. Inventory fullness uses the independently recovered carried-count/capacity rule in [the carry-capacity investigation](../research/damocles-carry-capacity.md#the-panel-gauge-is-why-the-count-is-doubled), while the browser presents the target level rather than claiming the original per-VBL gauge-cache timing.

Acceptance combines captured bit-plane comparisons in both buffers on foot, aboard and airborne, boundary and centre/palette tests, and deterministic browser screenshots for a maintainer's review under ADR 0011. The captured comparisons cover attitude, speed, altitude, clock and lamps; they do not establish parity for every centre, grid or space-navigation state. Raw captures stay Restricted Material; extracted payload remains governed by ADR 0029.
