# Define fidelity by Behavior Cases and governed departures

The Faithful Remake will demonstrate observable equivalence through versioned, evidence-linked Behavior Cases rather than emulator identity, inferred design intent, or an untestable claim that it merely feels the same. Each fidelity obligation retains its C64-derived expectation and Acceptance Envelopes, while a public Departure Record may accept a necessary least-divergent alternative—including a material one—as fully conformant after one maintainer reviews its evidence, impact, and tests.

## Consequences

Accepted departures never rewrite or remove the original baseline, and new Authoritative Evidence reopens affected cases and approvals. This permits practical host-platform or publication-driven alternatives without hiding them, at the cost of making full fidelity a governed evidence claim rather than a declaration that no material differences exist.
