# Render bounded Reference SID programs in an AudioWorklet

The Browser Shell renders the configured Reference Build's guarded, reachable SID programs through a project-owned bounded synthesizer shared by offline signal tests and an AudioWorklet. The independently versioned required `sound.json` carries the effective initial registers, one-shot programs, lift profiles, craft and Benson Display modulation rules, and stop controls; its version is presentation-only and never enters Save identity or frozen trace-facing manifest counts. The renderer implements only the registers, waveforms, oscillator sync and envelopes proven reachable by the sound decode, schedules ordered PresentationEvents against Gameplay Session elapsed time, and places one player-controlled master gain after the mix. Audio hardware or AudioWorklet failure degrades to silence with one Browser Shell notice, while an invalid or absent sound document refuses startup. The authored Arrival stays silent; its fast-forward path suppresses transient Sound Cues and reconstructs only final persistent state. This bounded owned path is chosen over procedural re-voicing, captured samples, a general C64 emulator, and a third-party SID core: those alternatives respectively invent behavior, distribute derived recordings, greatly exceed the reachable feature surface, or surrender the small fidelity boundary to an external runtime. Timing, interruption and overlap fidelity remain gated on the two-cold-run unwarped capture evidence and maintainer side-by-side listening recorded in `notes/decode/sound.md`.
