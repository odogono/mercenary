# Render bounded Reference SID programs in an AudioWorklet

The Browser Shell renders the configured Reference Build's guarded SID programs through a project-owned bounded synthesizer shared by offline signal tests and an AudioWorklet. Following the maintainer's revised audio target, acceptance means a recognisable, reasonably accurate rendition checked against isolated native-routine auditions, not chip-exact emulation or exhaustive two-cold-run gameplay proof. This owned path avoids invented procedural re-voicing, distributing captured samples, the scope of a general C64 emulator, and a third-party SID runtime dependency; the remaining evidence limitations and listening gate are explicit in `notes/decode/sound.md`.

The independently versioned required `sound.json` carries initial registers, one-shot programs, screen-change lift profiles, Lift Journey start/modulation rules, craft and Benson Display rules, and stops; it never enters Save identity or frozen trace-facing counts. The renderer supports the observed pulse, noise, triangle, ring modulation and envelopes. Lift Journey pitch and completion now follow the recovered 50 Hz phases; other documented output limits remain. Ordered PresentationEvents are scheduled against Gameplay Session elapsed time, with one master mute/volume surface after the mix. Invalid sound content refuses startup; unavailable browser audio degrades to silence with one notice. The authored Arrival remains silent, and skipping it suppresses transient Sound Cues before reconstructing final persistent state.

_(Amended 2026-09-25, issue #513: the AudioWorklet is now a **shared chip host**, not a SID-only processor. Damocles's YM2149 (#508) runs in the same host: `web/src/sound/workletHost.ts` queues timestamped batches, splits each render quantum at batch boundaries and, for Damocles, at its 50 Hz VBL driver ticks, and hosts any `Chip` that can write, render and reset. The SID synthesizer and its command vocabulary are unchanged. One behaviour now holds for both titles: a reset is applied **as it arrives**, not at its time. Before this, a reset sent while the context was suspended (Targ's ownership loss resets and then suspends) waited in the queue, and the resume's replace-pending message then threw it away, so a voice could outlive the reset. The presenters share their unlock, suspend and dispose lifecycle (`web/src/sound/lifecycle.ts`), and both boots use one `installAudioLifecycle` for the gesture unlock and for suspending and resuming on focus, visibility and the menu.)_
