# Give Targ an Enhanced sky behind a Presentation Preset

Targ gains an authored day/night orrery as presentation rather than Game Semantics: it is derived from elapsed session time, except when the Shell Console explicitly adjusts its presentation-only local time; it never changes play and is absent indoors. The adjustment belongs to the Browser Shell, is discarded with that shell's current game, and does not enter Session State, a Save, or a trace. Because the Reference Build has a fixed blue sky, the Browser Shell names the choice honestly as a Presentation Preset—Enhanced is the product default, while Authentic bypasses the orrery and preserves the accepted frame exactly—so the invention cannot quietly be mistaken for recovered behaviour.

The Enhanced preset owns a full deterministic solar and two-moon model, a seeded Star Sphere, and the colour of twilight. Its clock, latitude, obliquity, year and stars are settings for visual judgement; orbital elements, body sizes and colours remain one authored Targ composition rather than an unsupported public simulation surface.
