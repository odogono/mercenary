# Reconcile known taxi Saves with expanded campaign history

Accepted by the user, 2026-10-02, for [D2](../research/mercenary-iii-tourcraft-milestone-review.md) and [I4/#711](https://github.com/odogono/mercenary/issues/711). For valid late I2 taxi Saves and Session Resume, matching expanded campaign history takes priority over preserving the source cash and script state. [R8's counterexamples](../research/mercenary-iii-tourcraft-taxi-migration.md) establish that both requirements cannot hold after election/mining. Migration may change those fields where the recovered campaign requires it.

The [history-ambiguity follow-up](../research/mercenary-iii-tourcraft-history-ambiguity.md) additionally proves that two ordinary taxi histories produce identical valid old records but different expanded VM states. The user therefore accepted a **documented canonical expanded history for ambiguous late records**. Migration must deterministically select the same canonical reference for identical validated source state and compare its result and later behavior with uninterrupted expanded execution of that reference. Equality with the unknowable actual history is waived only for this ambiguous late domain. The canonical rule, its eligibility and its reconstruction must be documented and independently tested; accepting this policy does not certify an implementation or a particular reconstruction algorithm.

All 33 early taxi controls retain their full preservation, first-frame and expanded-future comparison requirements. Full source validation, the exact allowlisted identity, source-record preservation, isolated candidate validation and title-local Save/Session Resume ownership remain required for every migrated record. Canonicalization does not authorize fixed final cash/flags, campaign effects deferred until Load, resetting unrelated state, campaign disabling, a boarding deadline or blanket refusal of taxi records. The remaining R8/I4 gates are reconstruction and browser proofs under this accepted reference policy, rather than another decision about whether canonical history is permitted.
